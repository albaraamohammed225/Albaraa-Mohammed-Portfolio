مساعد HR الذكي لفرز السير الذاتيةAI HR Helper: CV Screening

Candidates upload a CV through a form; it's saved to Drive, analyzed by AI for a 0–100 score, summary and status, and logged in Google Sheets. An HR chat agent then answers questions about candidates and emails accepted ones automatically.
